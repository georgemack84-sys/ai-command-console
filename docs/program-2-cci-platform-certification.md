## Purpose

P2.18 certifies that the CCI platform is constitutionally qualified, evidence-backed, reproducible, governed, and ready for consumer adoption.

This phase converts P2.17 assurance outputs into formal platform certification decisions, platform certificates, certification reports, certification lifecycle records, and continuous certification monitoring.

Certification decisions are deterministic, evidence-backed, reproducible from immutable records, and issued only through approved constitutional authorities.

## Constitutional Authority

Authority ID: `P2.18-AUTH-INH-001`

P2.18 inherits authority from:

- Layer 0 Constitutional Certification Framework.
- Program Constitution.
- Constitutional Principles.
- Platform Governance.
- Platform Assurance.

P2.18 owns certification execution, certification governance, certification evidence, certification ledgering, and certification lifecycle management.

It does not create assurance evidence, redefine platform contracts, bypass governance, or certify uncertified capabilities.

## Platform Certification Framework

Framework ID: `P2.18-CERT-FWK-001`

The Platform Certification Framework defines the certification contract, model, rules, policies, scope, and boundaries.

Deliverables:

- Platform Certification Contract.
- Certification Model.
- Certification Policies.
- Certification Rules.
- Certification Scope.
- Certification Boundaries.

Rules:

- Certification is granted only after assurance is complete.
- Certification scope is explicit.
- Certification authority is recorded.
- Certification criteria are version governed.
- Certification boundaries are immutable for each certificate version.

## Certification Eligibility Engine

Eligibility Engine ID: `P2.18-ELIGIBILITY-ENGINE-001`

The Certification Eligibility Engine determines whether a platform capability or platform release may enter certification.

Eligibility requirements:

- Required phases complete.
- Required evidence available.
- Dependencies satisfied.
- Governance approved.
- Assurance complete.
- Replay validated.
- Platform contracts satisfied.

Deliverables:

- Certification Eligibility Report.
- Eligibility Findings.
- Eligibility Evidence References.

Failure behavior:

- Missing evidence blocks certification.
- Unresolved dependency conflicts block certification.
- Unapproved governance exceptions block certification.
- Failed assurance blocks certification.

## Exit Criteria

Exit Criteria ID: `P2.18-EXIT-CRITERIA-001`

P2.18 is complete when:

- Every platform capability has successfully passed Platform Assurance.
- Constitutional inheritance from Layer 0 is fully validated.
- All platform contracts, governance rules, and dependency contracts are satisfied.
- Certification evidence is complete, immutable, and replayable.
- Certification decisions are deterministic and reproducible.
- Platform certificates are issued through approved governance authorities.
- The Certification Ledger contains complete lifecycle history for every certification action.
- Certification reports are reproducible from immutable evidence.
- Continuous certification monitoring is operational.
- The Platform Certificate Registry is authoritative and synchronized with the Certification Ledger.
- The platform is constitutionally qualified and officially certified for consumer adoption.

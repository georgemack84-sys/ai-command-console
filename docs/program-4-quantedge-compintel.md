# Program 4 - Phase P4.12 QuantEdge CompIntel

P4.12 implements QuantEdge CompIntel (QCI) as the Civitas competitive intelligence application. QCI transforms governed sources, CCI evidence, CAF agent capabilities, replay references, and Program 4 application services into constitutional, explainable, replayable, evidence-backed strategic intelligence.

QCI owns competitive intelligence workflows, strategic intelligence generation, domain modeling, collection, analysis, synthesis, recommendation generation, visualization, reporting, and application orchestration. It does not own identity, namespaces, governance enforcement, replay infrastructure, evidence storage, telemetry infrastructure, certification infrastructure, constitutional authority, or policy enforcement.

## Implemented Artifacts

- `types/quantedge-compintel.ts` defines QCI foundation, domain registry, collection, analysis, synthesis, agent integration, explainability, dashboards, governance, readiness, qualification, certification, validation, scenarios, and bundle records.
- `services/quantedge-compintel/index.ts` provides deterministic run, validate, replay, and bundle functions.
- `app/api/quantedge-compintel/*` exposes authenticated contract, validation, foundation, domain, collection, analysis, synthesis, agent, evidence, dashboard, governance, readiness, and qualification projections.
- `tests/unit/quantedge-compintel/quantEdgeCompIntel.test.ts` validates doctrine, deterministic intelligence workflows, evidence-backed products, CAF integration, replay compatibility, qualification, and prohibited ownership boundaries.

## Exit Criteria Coverage

- QCI application foundation and architecture are present.
- Intelligence workflows are deterministic.
- Market, competitor, customer, technology, ecosystem, opportunity, and threat domains are registered.
- Collection consumes governed sources and CCI evidence with source qualification and provenance validation.
- Analysis covers trends, competitor comparison, SWOT, market positioning, opportunities, and threats.
- Synthesis produces explainable, evidence-backed strategic reports and recommendations.
- CAF Legion integration and governance gates are represented.
- Evidence citations, reasoning lineage, confidence explanations, and replay references are available.
- Dashboards and executive console are operational.
- Governance integration, operational readiness, replay compatibility, qualification evidence, qualification decision, and deployment certification are complete.

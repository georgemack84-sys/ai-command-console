# Noesis Phase 17 — Decision Learning

Decisions preserve contextual judgment evidence. A decision is not a universal rule, principle, or directive.

- Context, constraints, assumptions, alternatives, rejections, rationale, and tradeoffs travel with every decision.
- Rejections are local to the recorded decision.
- Outcomes are separate evidence and do not prove reasoning quality.
- Similarity is explained evidence only, never automatic reuse.
- Supersession is human-issued and preserves historical artifacts.
- Repeated decisions can form reviewable patterns only; they never create authority.

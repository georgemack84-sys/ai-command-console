# Authority × Confidence × Evidence — Phase 6, Part VIII

Every knowledge assessment retains three independent axes:

| Axis | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Authority | Who may establish it in this scope? |
| Confidence | How certain is the system? |
| Evidence | Why should it be believed? |

The typed profile contains no score-to-authority or evidence-to-authority
conversion. High confidence and multiple primary sources may coexist with an
unassessed authority axis. A recorded authority may coexist with low confidence.
Evaluation preserves all three axes and never grants execution permission.

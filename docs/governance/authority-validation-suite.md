# Authority Validation Suite — Phase 6, Part XIV

The Phase 6 suite validates the authority model through unit, conflict,
qualification, gate, audit, and adversarial tests. Its adversarial cases prove
that confidence near one, repeated evidence, and repeated observations do not
promote agent knowledge into human authority. They also prove that an
agent-derived record cannot become a human decision and that learning authority
never becomes action permission.

The tests are intentionally deterministic and local. They validate the
architectural invariants before future persistence, API, or UI integrations can
rely on the authority system.

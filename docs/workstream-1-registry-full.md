# W1.4B Registry Full

W1.4B expands Registry Core into the qualified Registry Platform for discovery, governance, dependency intelligence, compatibility validation, lineage analysis, lifecycle governance, contract validation, and infrastructure certification.

## Constitutional Scope

- Owns Registry Explorer, deterministic search, dependency intelligence, compatibility evaluation, registry lineage, lifecycle governance, contract validation, qualification evidence, and the Registry Infrastructure Gate.
- Consumes qualified Identity Full, Storage Full, Messaging Full, and Registry Core. Configuration, Observability, and Security Full are represented as explicit readiness controls until their dedicated W1 services land.
- Fails closed for invalid qualified predecessors, security failure, nondeterministic search, incompatible deployment allowance, unreplayable lineage, invalid contract allowance, governance or tenant isolation failure, audit integrity failure, or mutable qualification evidence.

## Qualification

The qualification suite verifies deterministic registry infrastructure qualification, explorer navigation, deterministic search, dependency validation, compatibility evaluation, lineage reconstruction, lifecycle governance, contract validation, immutable and replayable evidence, predecessor composition, conditional qualification, infrastructure gate failure, and fail-closed critical defects.

The canonical successful readiness decision is `QUALIFIED`, with final standing as the authoritative constitutional registry for the Civitas ecosystem.
